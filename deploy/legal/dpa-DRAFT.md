# InvisibleDB — Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

For business customers who process personal data through their InvisibleDB
instance. Attaches to the hosted Terms of Service.

## Roles

You are the **Controller** of personal data your application stores. We are the
**Processor**, acting only on your instructions (operating the instance,
backups, and support you request).

## Our commitments

1. **Instructions only.** We process personal data solely to provide the
   Service and as you instruct through your use of it.
2. **Confidentiality.** Staff with potential data access are bound by
   confidentiality obligations.
3. **Security.** Isolated per-customer instances, TLS in transit, encrypted
   backups, least-privilege internal access, nightly backups with tested
   restores.
4. **Subprocessors.** Listed in the Privacy Policy; we notify you 30 days
   before adding one. Current: Stripe, InterServer.
5. **Breach notice.** We notify you within 72 hours of discovering a personal
   data breach affecting your instance.
6. **Assistance.** We help you respond to data-subject requests (access,
   deletion, export) as they relate to our processing.
7. **Deletion.** On termination, after the 30-day export grace period, we
   delete your instance, its data, and its backups within 30 days.
8. **Audit.** Once per year you may request a written summary of our security
   practices; on-site audits by mutual agreement.

## International transfers

Processing currently occurs in the United States. If you transfer EU/UK
personal data to the Service, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914)
are incorporated by reference, with us as importer.

## Order of precedence

This DPA prevails over the Terms where they conflict on data protection.
